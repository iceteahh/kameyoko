KAMEYOKO

Fire a Kamehameha with your body. Duel a friend in a beam struggle.

  1. Stance — stand ~2 m back, feet wide, knees bent (power bonus).
  2. KA-ME-HA-ME… — cup both hands together and pull them back to your hip. Hold to charge.
  3. HA! — thrust both arms forward, wrists together, palms open.
  4. Duel — two players stand left & right. When beams collide, pump your arms to win the struggle.
